◆Clackwork

Set the pieces. Press Go. Ring the bell.

A chain-reaction puzzle with wooden toys. Each scene has a pusher, some dominoes, marbles and a brass bell. Put the few pieces in your tray on the empty spots, press Go and watch it clack along. Forty levels in five worlds, a new daily scene for everyone, no ads.

How to play

  • Tap a spot to place the selected tray piece. Tap it again to turn it around (ramps and springs) or take it back.
  • Press Go. The pusher starts the chain. Ring the bell to clear the scene.
  • Every rule is a fixed step, so the same setup always plays out the same way: dominoes topple into the next one (even one step down); marbles roll until they hit something and drop off ledges; ramps turn a falling marble or lift a rolling one; springs throw a marble two cells up and over; long dominoes reach two cells and bridge a gap.
  • Stars: one for the bell, one for ringing every chime, one for using no more pieces than par.
  • The daily scene is the same for everyone that day. Ring its bell every day to grow your streak.

Help

Peek. Stuck? Peek puts one piece of a working answer in place. Three peeks a day for everyone; a peeked scene earns at most two stars.

Didn't work? Your pieces stay where they were. Replay the run, watch where the chain stops, move a piece.

Restore Pro on a new iPhone. Settings › Restore purchase, signed in with the same Apple Account. Pro is a one-time purchase and works with Family Sharing.

Refunds. Apple handles payments and refunds: request a refund from Apple.

Move or back up your progress. Everything lives only on your iPhone and is included in your normal iPhone or iCloud backup. There is no account or server to restore it from.
